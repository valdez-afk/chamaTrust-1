How ChamaTrust Works

Simple steps to digitize your community savings group safely.

1

Register Your Group

Chama administrators create a group account and invite members using phone numbers or national IDs.

2

Automated M-Pesa Deposits

Members contribute funds via automated M-Pesa STK push or USSD prompts (*384*55#). Ledgers update instantly.

3

Dual-Key Multi-Sig Loans

Every loan payout requires dual digital sign-off from two authorized officials before funds disburse via B2C M-Pesa.
