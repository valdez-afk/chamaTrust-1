How ChamaTrust Works

From registering your chama to reviewing a new member, here is how ChamaTrust helps your group keep clear records and make fair decisions.

For Chama Administrators

  1. Create your account

    Register as an administrator with your name, email address, phone number and a password.

  2. Register your chama and members

    Add your chama and enter the details of each member so every person has a record.

  3. Record contributions and payouts

    Log what each member contributes and receives, so your group's history is kept in one place.

  4. Review trust scores and reported defaults

    See each member's trust score, calculated from the recorded data, and any default that has been reported.

  5. Check new members before admitting them

    Search for a prospective member across participating chamas and review their record before you decide.

For Chama Members

  1. Sign up

    Create a member account using your name, email address and phone number.

  2. View your record

    Sign in to see your own contributions, payouts and trust score.

  3. Ask for corrections

    If you find a mistake in your record, you can ask for it to be corrected.

About the Trust Score

The trust score is a summary of a member's recorded contributions and payouts. It helps administrators see a member's history quickly.

The score supports your decision; it should not make the decision for you. Records should be reviewed fairly, and a score alone should not decide whether a person is accepted into a chama or given a loan.
