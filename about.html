About ChamaTrust

ChamaTrust is a web-based system that helps Kenyan savings groups keep clear member records and make fair, informed decisions about who joins their chama.

The Problem We Address

Chamas are a trusted way for many households in Kenya to save and borrow outside formal financial institutions. They depend on trust, yet most groups keep their own records separately.

A member who defaults in one chama can join another with their history unchecked, because the new group has no way of verifying how that person behaved before. Administrators and members are left to carry the financial loss.

Our Solution

ChamaTrust gives participating chamas a shared place to record contributions and payouts, calculate a trust score for each member, and flag reported defaults.

Before admitting someone new, an authorised administrator can review the available record instead of relying on word of mouth.

What ChamaTrust Does

  • Records Contributions

    Administrators log member contributions and payouts so every chama has an organised record.

  • Calculates Trust Scores

    Each member receives a trust score based on the data recorded about their contributions.

  • Flags Defaults

    Reported defaults are flagged and made visible to other participating chamas.

  • Searches Across Chamas

    Administrators can look up a prospective member before making a membership decision.

Who It Is For

  • Chama Administrators

    Register your chama, manage members and review records before admitting new people.

  • Chama Members

    See your own record and ask for mistakes to be corrected.

  • New and Growing Chamas

    Verify a new participant's reliability instead of depending only on recommendations.

Our Principles

  • Fairness. Records support decisions; they should be reviewed fairly and never automatically decide whether someone is accepted or given a loan.
  • Transparency. Members can view their own record and request corrections.
  • Accountability. Clear, shared records help groups treat every member consistently.
  • Privacy. Only authorised chama administrators can review member records.
