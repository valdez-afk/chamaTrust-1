About ChamaTrust

Empowering informal savings groups across Kenya through secure, automated, and double-entry financial management.

Our Mission

To eliminate book-keeping discrepancies and fraud in informal Chamas by replacing manual paper ledgers with automated, multi-signature digital systems.

Our Vision

To build transparent financial trust for millions of community savings group members through seamless M-Pesa integration and accessible USSD interfaces.
