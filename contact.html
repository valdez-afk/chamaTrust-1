Common Questions

Who can see member records?

Only authorised chama administrators can review available member records. Members can see their own record.

What if my record has a mistake?

Send us a message using the form and choose “Request a record correction”. We will review it.

Does the trust score decide if I can join a chama?

No. The score supports an administrator’s decision, but it should not automatically decide whether someone is accepted or given a loan.

How do I register my chama?

Create an administrator account on the Register page, then add your chama and members.
