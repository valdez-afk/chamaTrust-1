Know Who You Are Admitting Before They Join Your Chama

ChamaTrust helps savings groups improve trust and accountability by keeping records of member contributions, payouts and financial obligations.

Authorised chama administrators can review available member records and identify reported defaults before making membership decisions.

Why Use ChamaTrust?

  • Review Member History

    Before admitting new members, administrators can search available records to make informed decisions.

  • Record Contributions

    Keep organised records of member contributions and payouts for easier reference.

  • Identify Reported Defaults

    View reported defaults to help assess a member's financial history.

  • Reduce Disagreements

    Use clear records to enhance transparency and accountability within your savings group.

Our Goal

Our goal is to help Kenyan savings groups make informed membership decisions and treat their members fairly.

Members can see their own record and ask for mistakes to be corrected.

ChamaTrust supports decision-making; its records should be reviewed fairly and not automatically determine whether a person is accepted into a chama or given a loan.
